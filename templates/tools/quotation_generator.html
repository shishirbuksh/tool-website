How is the grand total calculated?

Subtotal adds qty × rate per row. Tax adds each row amount × its tax % / 100. Grand total is subtotal plus tax. CGST+SGST splits tax in half for display.

When do I use CGST+SGST vs IGST?

Use CGST+SGST for intra-state sales and IGST for inter-state sales. Both use the same per-row tax %; only the label and split change.

Is my logo uploaded anywhere?

No. The logo is read locally with FileReader and previewed in your browser only. Share links contain form text and items, never the image.

How do share links work?

Copy Share Link serialises the quote to JSON, base64-encodes it after the #q= hash, and copies the URL. Opening that URL restores the same quote.
