Limits guide

What does each preset check?

  • Twitter 280 — counts characters including spaces and line breaks.
  • Meta 160 — ideal meta description length in characters.
  • SEO 1500 — counts words toward a typical in-depth article.
  • Essay 500 — counts words toward a short school essay.

The progress bar turns amber at 90% and red when you go over the limit.

How to use

Count words in 3 steps

  1. Paste or type into the big text box on the left.
  2. Watch live counts for words, characters, sentences and paragraphs.
  3. Pick a limit preset, tune the WPM slider, then copy or share.

Use the Sample button to try the tool, Clear to start over, and Copy Share Link to send text via URL hash.

FAQ

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time divides total words by your WPM slider value (100–400). Speaking time uses a fixed 130 WPM average.

How is keyword density calculated?

Top words of 3+ letters are counted case-insensitively. Density is count divided by total words. Toggle common words on or off.

Is my text uploaded?

No. All counting happens locally in your browser. Share links only store text in the URL hash.
