{{ lucide_icon("info","w-4 h-4",16)|safe }} Pricing Sources & Limitations

Sources and caching

Crypto reference prices come from the CoinGecko public API, with Binance public ticker data as a fallback. Fiat cross-rates come from ECB reference rates published through Frankfurter. Responses are cached for about 60 seconds. The status banner shows the source, timestamp and whether data is live, cached (stale) or unavailable.

No invented fallback prices

If upstream feeds fail, the converter does not substitute a benchmark or estimated price. It switches to manual mode and asks you to enter a rate. Manual results are labelled and are not live quotes.

Reference prices, not execution prices

Rates are mid-market references and may be delayed. Actual execution prices on centralized or decentralized exchanges differ because of order-book depth, spread, network fees and slippage.

Supported pairs

The live feed covers BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP, DOGE and ADA against USD and selected fiat currencies, plus ECB fiat crosses. You can enter any other pair and a manual rate. Always verify critical figures with your exchange or another independent source.
