{{ lucide_icon("shield","w-4 h-4",16)|safe }} Due-Diligence Tips

  • •Do your own research: Never rely on hype or a single checklist. Review the team, documentation, token distribution, audit scope and community history.
  • •Verify contract addresses: Get the contract address from the project's official website or a reputable explorer, not from unsolicited social-media messages. Compare every character before interacting.
  • •Check liquidity and permissions: Review lock status, lock duration, holder concentration and contract permissions on the relevant block explorer or analytics service. A lock is evidence to examine, not a guarantee.
  • •Confirm audits independently: Open the auditor's own report and check the contract address, date and scope. A logo on a project website is not proof of an audit.

{{ lucide_icon("alert-circle","w-4 h-4",16)|safe }} Limitations & Verification Notice

What this checklist does

It records red flags you identify yourself and gives each selected item a transparent, illustrative weight. The result is a prompt for further research, not a risk score produced from live on-chain data.

What this checklist does NOT do

It does not inspect smart-contract bytecode, simulate buys or sells, verify an audit, check wallet history, scan a blacklist or confirm whether a project is a scam. No project is verified or guaranteed safe because no flags were selected here.
