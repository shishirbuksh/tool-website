Privacy Policy

01 Scope and operator

StoryBrain AI is an independent online tools project operated at www.storybrainai.com. This policy covers this website and its tools. It does not cover third-party sites you reach through links or services you use separately.

Privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent to hello@storybrainai.com. We do not maintain a public postal address for this online-only project.

02 Tools that run in your browser

Many calculators, text formatters, converters, generators and checklists execute in JavaScript in your browser. Amounts, text, generated passwords and checklist selections used by those tools are not uploaded to our servers.

Some browser tools save convenience data in localStorage, such as your theme, recent calculation history or conversion history. That data stays on your device until you clear it or your browser removes it.

Page-view analytics still applies. Even when a tool runs locally, the page shell may send the anonymous analytics event described below. The tool's inputs are not included.

03 Server-assisted tools

Some image and PDF utilities send the file you select to our backend for processing. Examples include image background removal, watermarking, compression or conversion, and PDF merge or conversion tools. Our application code reads the upload into memory, performs the operation and returns the result. We do not intentionally save these uploads or outputs to application storage or a user account.

Server infrastructure may still record request metadata (for example the URL, time, IP address and response status) in access logs. It does not record the binary contents of the file. Do not upload documents containing information you are not permitted to send to a third-party service.

The Crypto Price Converter requests a generic rate table from public crypto and fiat reference APIs. Your amount and selected pair are not sent to those APIs. The Crypto Red Flag Self-Assessment runs entirely in the browser and does not inspect addresses, wallets, contracts or on-chain data.

04 Data we process

Server access logs

Our Caddy web server writes JSON access logs. These normally include the request time, requested host and path, HTTP status, response size, IP address, user agent, referrer and request duration. We use them for security, abuse prevention, rate limiting and troubleshooting.

Logs rotate at 100 MB, five files are kept, and rotated files are deleted after about seven days under the current configuration. A very busy period may shorten that window.

First-party page-view analytics

We record a small first-party event when a tool page loads. The analytics database stores only the event name (normally the tool slug), the event category (normally page_view) and a timestamp. It does not store your IP address, user agent, account, amount or file in that database. Events older than 90 days are deleted automatically.

Contact messages

If you use the contact form, you provide a name, email address and message. The message is sent through our configured SMTP relay to our inbox so we can reply. Our server log also records the submitted name, email address and the first 80 characters of the message for abuse investigation. Contact emails remain in the mailbox until no longer needed or until you ask us to delete them, subject to legal or security requirements.

Optional AI providers

If you choose a third-party provider in the NFT generator, the prompt you enter and the API key you supply are transmitted through our server to that provider (OpenAI, DeepSeek or Google, depending on your choice) so it can return generation parameters. Those providers handle the data under their own terms. Use the local provider if you do not want to send a prompt to a third party, and never submit a key you are not authorised to use.

Data we do not ask for

  • We do not require an account, password or payment card for normal tool use.
  • We do not ask for postal addresses or government identifiers.
  • We do not intentionally retain uploaded files, generated passwords or tool inputs in application storage.

05 Cookies, analytics and advertising

The site uses browser storage for basic functionality: your theme choice, cookie consent choice and, in some tools, recent history. These values are stored locally and are not used to build a profile on our server.

Google Analytics and AdSense

Google Analytics and Google AdSense are loaded only after you accept non-essential cookies. Google may use cookies or similar identifiers to measure traffic and, where personalised ads are enabled, to serve ads based on prior visits. If you reject consent, those scripts are not loaded.

  • You can change your choice with the Cookie Settings button below or in your browser storage controls.
  • You can manage Google ad personalisation at Google Ads Settings.
  • Industry opt-out tools are available at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.
Your cookie choices:

Clicking Cookie Settings clears the stored consent value and reloads the page so you can choose again. Rejecting consent prevents Google Analytics and AdSense from loading.

Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends a GPC signal, this site treats it as a rejection of non-essential analytics and advertising cookies. The consent banner is suppressed, Google scripts are not loaded, and a previously stored “accepted” choice is overridden.

06 Third-party services

Some features rely on external services. Their operators may receive technical request data such as your IP address, user agent and the requested endpoint, even when we do not send your tool input.

  • Google LLC — Google Analytics and Google AdSense, loaded only after consent and blocked when GPC is enabled.
  • CoinGecko and Binance — public crypto reference prices requested by our server, not by your browser with your amount or pair.
  • Frankfurter / European Central Bank — public fiat reference rates requested by our server.
  • Alternative.me — public Crypto Fear & Greed index data requested by our server.
  • OpenAI, DeepSeek or Google — only if you actively choose that provider in the NFT generator; your prompt and supplied API key are sent to the selected provider.

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. We do not control their privacy practices; review their policies before sharing information with them.

07 Retention and security

Data Retention
Caddy access logs Up to about 7 days or 5 rotated files, whichever comes first
First-party analytics events 90 days, then deleted automatically
Contact emails Until handled or deleted on request, subject to legal/security needs
Browser localStorage Until you clear it or your browser removes it

The site is served over HTTPS through Caddy and uses security headers such as HSTS, a content security policy with per-request nonces, and frame and MIME-sniffing protections. No internet service can promise absolute security, but we limit collection and keep retention short.

Do not send sensitive personal, financial or medical documents through a tool unless you understand and accept the processing described here. For highly sensitive material, use an offline tool you control.

08 Your choices and rights

You can clear local storage and cookies through your browser at any time. You can reject or withdraw advertising and analytics consent using the Cookie Settings button. If GPC is available in your browser, enable it to send an automatic rejection signal.

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA apply, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not operate user accounts, we may not be able to identify you from analytics or logs; we will explain what can and cannot be actioned.

To make a request, email hello@storybrainai.com with enough information to identify the relevant contact message or log entry. We do not sell personal information, and we do not knowingly process data from children under 13.
