How we create content

  • Checked with care: tool pages explain their inputs and limits. Where tax, legal or financial points are cited, the page links to the source and shows a review date.
  • AI-assisted drafting: some drafts may use AI for structure. Published figures and examples are checked against the linked sources where applicable. Report errors via contact — we correct and note the update date.
  • Original explanations: we aim to write our own copy, formulas and examples rather than copying other sites. Similar tools are described with their own limits and guides.

Ongoing curation

  • Updated: tool and guide pages carry last-updated dates. Finance, tax and health pages link to the disclaimer and are educational only.
  • Monitored: contact inbox hello@storybrainai.com is reviewed; privacy requests can be sent to the same address.
  • Transparent ads: the site is funded by Google AdSense (consent-gated). Ad choices are explained in privacy.
