02 Cryptocurrency & Financial Disclaimers

Digital asset markets are characterized by extreme volatility, regulatory shifts, and high risk of capital loss.

  • No Investment Advice: None of our crypto price converters, halving countdowns, DCA calculators, fear & greed trackers, or mining calculators constitute investment or financial recommendations.
  • Reference Prices: Crypto converter rates are mid-market references from public APIs, cached for about 60 seconds. They are not executable quotes and do not include spread, fees or slippage. When feeds fail, the tool switches to manual mode rather than inventing a price.
  • Manual Scam Checklist: The crypto red-flag tool records warning signs you select yourself. It does not inspect contracts, wallets, liquidity or blacklists, and a zero score is not a guarantee that a project is safe.
  • Speculative Nature: Any historical data, technical indicators, or projection formulas represent simplified mathematical models and cannot predict future market events.
  • Risk of Loss: Never trade, purchase, or invest capital that you cannot afford to lose entirely. You bear full sole responsibility for any trades or investments made.

04 Accuracy & Technical Limitations

We engineer our tools to minimize errors, yet technical factors beyond our control may influence results:

  • Floating-Point Arithmetic: Client-side JavaScript floating-point representations can introduce minor rounding nuances in extreme decimal calculations.

  • Client Device Variability: Browser engine implementations, hardware acceleration profiles, and local system clocks can occasionally impact execution performance.
