AI & Crypto tools — estimators, not advisers

This hub groups price, trend, portfolio, tax and risk estimators that run on public market math in your browser. Each tool states its inputs and limits up front: moving averages and volatility bands describe past price action, they do not predict future returns. Use DCA and portfolio views to compare allocations side by side, then verify tax figures with a qualified professional before filing.

Start with Crypto Price Prediction for scenario bands, Crypto Trend Analyzer for momentum context, and Crypto Tax Calculator for gain estimates. All three link to step-by-step blog guides with methodology and update dates.

Finance calculators here show full amortization, fee and tax inputs — not just a monthly number. EMI, SIP, FD, GST and mortgage views expose formulas, prepayment effects and effective rates so you can reproduce every figure. Figures are educational estimates; confirm with your bank or adviser.
