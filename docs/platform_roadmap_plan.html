Architectural Invariants & Non-Negotiable Standards

Invariant #1 • Privacy Boundary
100% Client-Side Computation
StoryBrain AI will never transmit calculation inputs, passwords, base64 images, or PDF bytes to backend servers. All compute happens inside browser RAM. The server acts exclusively as an air-gapped distributor of static assets and SSR templates.
Invariant #2 • Design Purity
Cal.com Aesthetic Consistency
All tools must use the Cal.com aesthetic: high contrast obsidian/white themes, hairline 1px borders, subtle surface elevations, 12px+ legible typography, and 3-dot window frame headers. No third-party marketing clutter or fake badge elements permitted.
Invariant #3 • Quality & Resilience
Zero-Breakage Automated Verification
No commit may be pushed to origin/main without passing the complete 150-test pytest suite. Production deployments to the Hostinger CloudPanel VPS must remain deterministic with automatic state synchronization and systemctl service reloading.
